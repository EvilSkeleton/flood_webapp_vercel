About the model

Floods caught at the 40% threshold
9Predictors (data dictionary selection)
1,000Training observations, 9 basins

Trained on the synthetic sample dataset supplied with the case (Flood_Prediction_v2.1). Outcome fields (water depth, NDWI, risk score, severity) were excluded to prevent leakage. The threshold is set at 40% rather than 50% because a missed flood costs lives and a false alarm costs trust. Field accuracy will be lower than on synthetic data; this is a prototype, not an operational warning system.
